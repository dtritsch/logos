# Welcome to The Logos Hub

This digital archive serves as an independent study hub and dynamic portfolio of my research in **logic, rhetoric, and applied intellect**.

## Research Architecture

* **[Master Syllabus](/syllabus/):** The 8-phase hybrid curriculum spanning formal logic, the historical rhetorical canon, and strategic systems.
* **Reading Vault:** 300-word critical syntheses of foundational primary texts.
* **Logic & Case Studies:** Deconstructions of legal arguments, public discourse, and formal proofs.
* **Monographs:** Long-form capstone treatises synthesizing logic, rhetoric, and modern cognitive discourse.
