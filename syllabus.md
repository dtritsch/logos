# Logic, Rhetoric, and Applied Intellect
### Master Curriculum & Roadmap

## Overview of Program Phases

* **Phase 1:** Formal Logic, Propositional Calculus & Cognitive Heuristics
* **Phase 2:** Historical Survey of Rhetorical Tradition
* **Phase 3:** Classical Antiquity (Plato, Aristotle, Cicero, Quintilian, Augustine)
* **Phase 4:** Enlightenment & Philosophy of Language (Bacon, Locke, Campbell)
* **Phase 5:** 20th-Century Revival (Burke, Perelman, Toulmin, Richards)
* **Phase 6:** Modern Critical Discourse & Cognitive Metaphor (Lakoff, Foucault, Jasinski)
* **Phase 7:** Multidisciplinary Applied Systems (Legal Reasoning, Game Theory, Crisis PR)
* **Phase 8:** Capstone Doctoral-Level Treatise & Logic Mapping
